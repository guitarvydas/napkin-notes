TDPL stands for "Top Down Parsing Language".  Most common parsers, like YACC, are bottom up - I'm inferring that this might be called BUPL for "Bottom Up Parsing Language". TDPLs allow parsing context sensitive languages, whereas BUPLs tend to parse context free languages (CFGs).

In my opinion, context sensitive languages feel to be more expressive and more humane. CFGs make the trade off towards technology - restricted by what could be easily analyzed in the 20th century - vs. human level expressivity.

If one wanted to parse context sensitive languages, one might look at [packrat parsing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packrat_parser) associated with PEG parsing technologies. 

I, also, tend to think that the definition of "slow" in the 21st century is different from the 20th century definition.

With 21st century hardware and software, we might wish to reevaluate the trade offs.
