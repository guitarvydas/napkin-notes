It's not that this new medium will turn people like me into artists, it's that artists will figure out a way to create art using this new medium. I'm a dyed in the wool screaming, blues guitarist (Winter, Hendrix, Trower, ...) and have never accepted synths nor auto-tune. Yet, something like a decade after their appearance someone (else) figured out how to make those thing palatable. Anecdote: a young teen that I wrote with was accused of using auto-tune when in fact she was just mimicking auto-tune with precise control of her own voice. (I wouldn't bother to learn that skill, because I've been trained to dislike that sound). Yes, good artists won't share, not because they don't want to but because they _can't_ - they will intuit the result and can't be introspective enough to explain how they do that.
