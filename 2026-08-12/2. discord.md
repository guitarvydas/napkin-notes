In general they discuss compiler building and language dev on that channel and might be of general interest. [I just typed this prompt into ChatGPT: "what does gingerBill have to say about using LLVM for compiler building?"]
I'm advocating treating _language_ design as a separable entity from _compiler_ building. Bloatiness matters most when one is trying to Production Engineer a compiler, which is different from designing a language. From my perspective, language design is generally overlooked treated in a waterfall manner. We then wait years for comments and suggestions for years from people who actually use the language (instead of getting that stuff out of the way much earlier in the process).
