> Very interesting how the PEG rules are 1:1 with grammar in a way that isn't guaranteed with CFG's (although many CFG's seem to disambiguate to that point with ooo/associativity/etc.)

It appears to me that the backtracking in PEG is the big win. I write a grammar rule and don't have to worry about how to mangle it to appease a parser generator. In 1960s, backtracking was taboo due to slowness of hardware, but this is no longer the case.
