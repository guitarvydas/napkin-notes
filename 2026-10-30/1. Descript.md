I'm growing progressively more annoyed with the egregious UX changes in Descript.

I use the tool only lightly to knock off little (technical) explainer videos about once a week. The main features I want are (a) studio sound and (b) the ability to delete parts of the video (manually - I gave up on trying to use Underlord to filter out long spaces and filler words - it broke something and I didn't have interest in debugging your product, I had work that I wanted to get done without being side-tracked). 

Every week, I use a new tool which happens to have the same name "Descript". This causes me to slow down while learning how to do something I re-learned a week ago.

I'm a very technical, software person, but I'm just a user of Descript. I want Descript to do something for me that I don't want to take the time to learn (e.g. edit videos). When I figure out how to do something, I want to reuse that knowledge and speed up my workflow. Instead, I have to learn something new almost every time I use Descript.

I'm on a Mac. Today, I had to login again (even though Descript was still open from last week) and the cursor flickers and selection does strange things when I edit text.

In my opinion, Continuous Deployment is a bane to society - it seems to relieve companies from actually getting a product Design right and QA'ing it, knowing that they can update on a whim at any time in the future and knowing that they can expect paying users to suggest improvements. I used to use Linux and Android and then switched to an Apple-only environment due to Android's and Linux' excessive featurism and Android's overuse of continuous deployment.

I chose Descript many years ago because it seemed to do what I wanted. Now, I just want stability, not more technical features. I want a desktop app that doesn't change its UX and doesn't auto-update itself unless I ask it to. I don't want to read change logs and "what's new" documents. I just want to get my work done in as little time as possible.
