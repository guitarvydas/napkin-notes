[After reading this](https://metacognitionai.substack.com/p/you-cant-undo-spilled-coffee?selection=e03004ed-0afd-43a4-bc1b-dd9983985474&utm_campaign=post-share-selection&utm_medium=web&aspectRatio=instagram&textColor=%23ffffff&bgImage=true) I get the overwhelming feeling that we can't create autonomous robots without resorting to a huge number of parallel processors and that our current attempts at describing / expressing these issues solely using the functional paradigm are doomed. We need to spend a decade or so letting an LLM learn how to cope with these problems, then use a Vulcan mind-meld to snapshot and duplicate all of the parallel bits of learning, or, we just need to lower our sights and try to achieve only small bits of helpful tooling. Our human minds don't understand how human minds work, making the notion that we could duplicate human behaviour using little recipes, moot.
