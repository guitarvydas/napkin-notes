Why do we continually emphasize performance and efficiency?

These biases were formed in the mid-20th century when our computer hardware was still infantile and relatively slow and expensive.

At the time, we decided that we needed to trade off human time in order to save CPU  cycles and memory footprint.

At the time, we could only afford single CPUs and we could get away with techniques that involved sharing memory. We didn't notice that shared memory caused a large amount of headaches that we classify as "thread safety". Programming languages and operating systems that we developed under those assumptions. The footprint of programming languages developed under such biases is much larger (orders of magnitude larger) than if languages were developed without those constraints.
