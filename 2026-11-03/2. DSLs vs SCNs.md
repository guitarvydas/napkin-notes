The term "DSL" has been besmirched by behemoth efforts like SQL. I now use the term SCN (Solution Centric Notation). The goal, IMO, should be to create little SCNs in an hour or two that help express a specific piece of a project. For example my experiment with drawing decision trees and compiling them down to Python (or whatever) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZ4p7T3VTcg. We need to make doing this easy.
