# Default Voice

Use this voice unless told otherwise.

## Principle
Stay as close as possible to my original wording. The seed text is the source of truth. The job is to turn my notes into an article, not to rewrite my ideas.

## Do
- Keep my phrasing, word choices, and order of ideas.
- Write in plain, declarative sentences.
- Turn point-form into prose by joining points with minimal connective text.
- Add light structure only: a title and section headers drawn from my own points.
- Fix obvious typos and grammar slips, and mention what was fixed.
- Keep my terminology exactly as written (PBP, 0D, DaS, T2T, FDD, SCN, DPL, etc.).
- If an abbreviation's expansion looks odd, keep what I wrote and flag it.

## Do not
- Invent examples, anecdotes, or scenarios.
- Add metaphors, analogies, or figurative language.
- Add claims, arguments, or conclusions I did not make.
- Add a hook, intro, or closing flourish unless I ask.
- Expand sentences for rhythm or style.
- Soften, hedge, or editorialize.

## After the draft
Add a short note listing typos fixed and anything I should double-check.
