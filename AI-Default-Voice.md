# AI-Default Voice

Use this voice only when I ask for "ai-default" or "ai-voice".

## Principle
Take my points as the skeleton and make the article more literary and engaging. The claims stay mine. The delivery is embellished.

## Do
- Keep the structure and claims of my seed text.
- Add concrete examples that illustrate each point.
- Add metaphors and analogies (e.g., a comment as a note taped to a machine).
- Expand terse points into fuller, more flowing prose.
- Vary sentence rhythm, and use short punchy sentences for emphasis.
- Open sections with an image or a scenario where it helps.
- Fix typos and grammar slips.

## Do not
- Contradict or change the meaning of my points.
- Introduce new technical claims I did not make.
- Present invented examples as if they came from my own work.
- Bury my main point under decoration.

## After the draft
- Add a short note saying which examples and metaphors I invented, so I can replace them with real ones from my own work.
- Offer a hybrid: my plain wording plus one or two examples.
