> Despite this possibility, I am of the opinion that it is better to use diagrams for what they are good at and to use text for what it is good at.

> A reasonable _simplifying notation_ should not be an attempt to cover every possible use-case (Turing-completeness is over-rated at the code snippet level).

> There are several kinds of control flow at this level: looping, recursion, feedback, if-elif (no else)

> 21st century hardware is very different from what we had in the last century. IMO, it is time to re-think our approach to using this new kind of hardware.
