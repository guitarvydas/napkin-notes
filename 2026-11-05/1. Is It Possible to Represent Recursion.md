> Can you represent recursion with this ? also mutual recursion.

While it might be possible, I am of the opinion that it is better to use diagrams for what they are good at and to use text for what it is good at.

A reasonable _simplifying notation_ should not be an attempt to cover every possible use-case (Turing-completeness is over-rated at the code snippet level).

There are three at least kinds of control flow at this level:
1. looping
2. recursion
3. feedback

Looping and feedback are not well handled in text (not impossible, just less expressive to human readers). They are better handled in diagrammatic form. Drakon for looping, PBP and message-passing for feedback.

Recursion is better handled using a textual notation. 

We are forced to learn recursion because, in the 20th century, we were stuck with machines that could conveniently handle 500-year old, Gutenberg type-setting notations, but were too weak to handle diagrams, and because we (incorrectly, IMO) thought that mathematic, type-set notation would be the best (and the only) way to write programs for controlling computers.
